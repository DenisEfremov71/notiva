Delete your Notiva account

Notiva is developed by Denis Efremov. You can delete your Notiva account and all of its data at any time.

Option 1: In the app

  1. Open Notiva and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: By email

If you can't access the app, email support@denkana.com from the email address on your Notiva account, with the subject "Notiva account deletion". We will confirm the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record and email address
  • All notes and content you created in Notiva

Deletion is permanent and can't be undone. We don't keep any of your data after your account is deleted.

See our Privacy Policy for more details.
