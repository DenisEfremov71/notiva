Notiva Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Notiva ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. Notiva is developed by Denis Efremov, based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. By using Notiva, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password when you create an account. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are stored in hashed form. We never see your password.
  • Your notes: the notes and other content you create in Notiva. These are stored in the cloud so they can sync across your devices.
  • Technical information: to keep sign-in secure and the service working, Firebase may process limited technical data such as your IP address, a unique user ID, and basic device information.

Notiva does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or other personal data from your device.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To store your notes and sync them across your devices.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To respond to you when you contact us for support.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your notes for advertising or profiling.

3. Service providers

Notiva uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), provided by Google LLC, to handle accounts and store data. Firebase processes data on our behalf under Google's terms. Your data may be stored and processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Learn more in Firebase's privacy information.

We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it, or unless we need to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or others.

4. Data security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS, and is encrypted at rest by Firebase. Access to your notes is restricted to your account. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account information and notes for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: open Settings and choose Delete account.
  • On the web: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

When you delete your account, your email address, account record, and all of your notes are permanently deleted from our systems. Deletion requests sent by email are completed within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can update or delete most information directly in the app. For any other request, email us at support@denkana.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's privacy

Notiva is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we may also let you know in the app.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at support@denkana.com.
